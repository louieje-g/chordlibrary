            Tap your profile icon to access Preferences — choose theme (dark/light) and chord notation (sharp # or flat b).

            ↕️
            
          
            Playlist Reorder
            
          
            Drag the ⋮⋮ handle on playlist songs to reorder them. Long-press the handle on mobile.
            
        
            
        
            
          
            📱
            
          
            Smarter QR Sharing
            
          
            QR codes are now lighter — lyrics are stripped, keeping only chords and structure for easier scanning.
